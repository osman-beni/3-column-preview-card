Sedans

Practical, efficient, and comfortable for everyday driving.

Why should you choose a sedan?

Sedans are a great choice when you want a comfortable vehicle that's easy to drive around town while still being suitable for longer journeys.

Best for

  • Daily commuting
  • City driving
  • Road trips
  • Small families

Typical features

  • Good fuel economy
  • Comfortable passenger space
  • Large trunk
  • Easy handling
